Privacy Policy

Smart University Portal · Last updated 26 November 2026

Smart University Portal ("the portal") is a student portal for Metropolitan University. This page explains what data the portal uses, including data from Google when a student chooses to connect Google Classroom.

Portal account data

When you sign up, the portal stores your name, email address, academic profile (such as student ID, department and batch) and the records you create or that the university adds for you, such as enrolments, attendance, results and fee payments. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth; the portal never sees or stores your password.

Google Classroom connection (optional)

Connecting Google Classroom is optional. If you connect it, the portal asks Google for read-only access to:

  • your Google account email address and account ID, to show which Google account is connected;
  • the Classroom classes you belong to;
  • your own coursework and submissions in those classes (titles, due dates, status, points and grades);
  • the topics in those classes, to label lab work.

The portal uses this data only to show you your own upcoming, missing and turned-in Classroom work inside the portal. It cannot create, change, submit or delete anything in Google Classroom. To submit work, the portal opens the item in Google Classroom itself.

Classroom coursework is fetched live from Google each time you open the page and is not stored by the portal. The only Google data stored is your Google email address, account ID and the access and refresh tokens Google issues, which are kept encrypted in the portal's database.

The portal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Removing the Google connection

Press Disconnect on the portal's Google Classroom page to delete the stored Google tokens and email address. You can also remove the portal's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who can see your data

Your portal records are visible to you and to authorised university staff (teachers and administrators) as needed for their role. Your Google Classroom data is shown only to you.
